Background

I hold a BS in Management Sciences from NED University of Engineering & Technology, with a specialization in Supply Chain Management. My academic focus centered on procurement, logistics, and the operational systems that keep supply chains running efficiently.

I currently work as a Procurement Officer at Hemani International, where I support day-to-day procurement activities and gain hands-on exposure to Microsoft Dynamics 365. This follows earlier exposure to SAP ERP during an operations internship at Pak Suzuki, which gave me a practical view of how large organizations manage operations and supply processes.

I take an analytical, data-driven approach to problem-solving and I'm continuing to build my knowledge of supply chain analytics and process improvement, with a genuine interest in how organizations can make procurement and logistics more efficient and sustainable.
